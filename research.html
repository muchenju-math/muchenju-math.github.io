Coefficient Positivity for Arborescence Ratios of Covering Digraphs

Muchen Ju, Junjie Ni, Kaixin Wang, and Yihan Xiao (α−β).

arXivLean formalization
Summary

For a directed graph with a chosen root, the arborescence polynomial sums the products of edge weights over all spanning trees directed toward that root. In a finite graph cover, each lifted edge inherits the variable of its base edge. At corresponding roots, the ratio of the cover's arborescence polynomial to the base polynomial is known to be an integer polynomial. We prove that every coefficient of this ratio is nonnegative, resolving the positivity conjecture of Pylyavskyy.

Arborescences of Random Covering Graphs

Muchen Ju, Junjie Ni, Kaixin Wang, and Yihan Xiao (α−β).

FPSAC 2025arXiv
Summary

A rooted arborescence of a directed graph is a spanning tree oriented toward a chosen vertex. Building on work of Chepuri et al. connecting arborescences of a directed graph \(G\) with those of its covering graphs, this paper studies the weighted total of arborescences in a random covering graph. It derives a formula for the expected weighted sum and thereby resolves a conjecture posed by Chepuri et al.

Majority Dynamics on Resampled Sparse Erdős–Rényi Graphs: Gaussian Winner Selection and Pace to Unanimity

Ioana Dumitriu, Muchen Ju, and Hai-Xiao Wang (α−β). Submitted.

arXiv
Summary

This paper studies two-opinion majority dynamics when the interaction graph is independently resampled at every step from the sparse Erdős–Rényi model \(\mathbb{G}(N,p)\), with \(p=b\log N/N\) and fixed \(b>1\). Writing \(B_t\) and \(R_t\) for the blue and red camps at time \(t\), the behavior is governed by the initial blue advantage \(\Delta_0=|B_0|-|R_0|\).

The results identify three regimes. An initial blue advantage above an explicit constant multiple of \(N/\sqrt{\log N}\) yields blue unanimity within two updates with high probability. In the intermediate range \(\sqrt{N/\log N}\ll\Delta_0\lesssim N/\sqrt{\log N}\), the paper gives explicit high-probability upper and lower bounds on the time to blue unanimity.

In the critical window \(\Delta_0\sqrt p=O(1)\), the eventual winner has an asymptotically Gaussian selection law:

\[ \Pr(\text{blue unanimity}) = \Phi\!\left(\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\,\Delta_0\sqrt p\right)+o(1), \] \[ \Pr(\text{red unanimity}) = \Phi\!\left(-\sqrt{\frac{2}{\pi}}\,\Delta_0\sqrt p\right)+o(1). \]

In this critical regime, unanimity is reached within \((1+o(1))\log N/\log\log N\) updates with high probability.

Majority Dynamics on Assortative Sparse Stochastic Block Models

Ioana Dumitriu, Muchen Ju, and Hai-Xiao Wang (α−β).

arXiv
Summary

This work studies majority dynamics on a resampled sparse binary stochastic block model in the assortative regime. At each update, the graph is redrawn from the current opinion partition: same-opinion vertices connect with probability \(\alpha=a\log N/N\), while opposite-opinion vertices connect with probability \(\beta=b\log N/N\), with \(a>b>1\).

Writing \(B_t\) and \(R_t\) for the blue and red camps at time \(t\), the analysis shows that the weighted advantage \(\widetilde{\Delta}_t=b|B_t|-a|R_t|\), rather than only the ordinary difference \(\Delta_t=|B_t|-|R_t|\), controls the time to unanimity. With high probability as \(N\to\infty\), the paper identifies constant-time, subpolynomial-time, and polynomial-time regimes for reaching blue unanimity from an initial blue majority.

In particular, blue unanimity occurs within three updates when \(\widetilde{\Delta}_0\gtrsim-N/\sqrt{\log N}\), and within \(N^{o(1)}\) updates when \(\widetilde{\Delta}_0<0\) but \(|\widetilde{\Delta}_0|=o(N)\). In the polynomial regime the exponent is governed by

\[ I_0= \left( \mathbf{ReLU}\!\left( \sqrt{a\frac{|R_0|}{N}}- \sqrt{b\frac{|B_0|}{N}} \right) \right)^2, \qquad \mathbf{ReLU}(x)=\max\{x,0\}. \]

The proof develops precise estimates for one-vertex flip probabilities involving sparse binomial differences, which may also be useful beyond this model.

Sharp Inner Product Correlations for Hypercube Bijections

Muchen Ju and Ijay Narang (α−β).

arXiv
Summary

The paper proves a conjecture of Rob Morris about bijections of the hypercube. For every bijection \(f:\{-1,1\}^n\to\{-1,1\}^n\), it establishes

\[ \Pr_{x,y\in\{-1,1\}^n}\!\left[ \langle x,y\rangle\ge 0\ \text{and}\ \langle f(x),f(y)\rangle\ge 0 \right] \ge \frac14-O\!\left(\frac1{\sqrt n}\right). \]

The argument uses the spectral decomposition of the Hamming association scheme to recast the problem as a linear program over the Birkhoff polytope. The nonprincipal spectral contribution is shown to be asymptotically negligible, leaving the principal eigenvalue as the dominant term.
